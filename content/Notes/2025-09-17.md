- We started at 2am from the O'hara parking lot.
- Our route was to ascend Mount Victoria via Abbot Pass, since the SE ridge seemed like a fun route.
- We were slowed down by snow and ice travel and some small difficulty route-finding. Not to mention the significant distance and elevation gain.
- We descended down to the Huber glacier, which was probably a faster exit, despite the additional glacier hazard. It wasn't a trivial glacier.
- We were hoping to get back in time for the 4:30 bus out from O'hara, but that ended up slipping until 6:30 when we realized that the ridge was taking longer than expected.
- Despite trail-running down from Wiwaxy Gap, the bus had no free seats and we had to hike out the additional 11km, finishing at 9pm.
- I forgot to swap my insoles into my mountaineering boots, and the lack of insoles made for a painful day.
