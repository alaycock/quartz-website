This one is marked as "worthwhile" in the Don't Waste Your Time book, but I'd rate it lower. Forking off the Taylor Lake trail immediately led to bushwhacking and while it wasn't the worst bushwhacking, the resulting views were only "pretty good". I imagine the views from Panorama Peak or Panorama Ridge or would be much more worthwhile.
