I'm Adam, I'm a software developer and someone who enjoys spending time in the Canadian Rockies. If you're interested in my professional experience, check out my [[assets/Adam Laycock - Resume.pdf|resume]].

I built this site to keep track of the trips I've done and to share them with others. A lot of my trip motivations are based off of lists that other folks have curated, and this site aims to keep track of those lists, and my progress towards them. This is why you'll find separate links to both the trip reports, but also the "routes" themselves which are mostly empty. One page is to track a specific trip, and the other to track details about the route.

This site is built using [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/), which is a neat way to manage a website through [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/). I welcome contributions and fixes through the [Github repo](https://github.com/alaycock/quartz).
